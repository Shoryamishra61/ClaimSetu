# Problem verdict

## 1. Final chosen problem

An EPFO member whose previous PF account is unavailable for online transfer cannot see, at the point of failure, that the previous employment's missing Date of Exit is the prerequisite they must resolve before retrying.

## 2. Exact citizen

A salaried worker who has changed jobs, has an old EPFO Member ID under the same UAN, and wants to transfer the old balance to the current account.

## 3. Exact triggering situation

The citizen opens the online transfer journey and the previous PF account is missing or cannot proceed.

## 4. Current journey

1. Sign in to the Member Portal.
2. Start an online transfer.
3. Fail to find or select the previous account.
4. Inspect service history, search help, ask the former employer or search the web.
5. Discover that Date of Exit is absent.
6. Determine whether self-service Mark Exit is available or employer/field-office help is required.
7. Update the official record, wait for it to appear, and retry transfer.

## 5. Exact failure

Official guidance contains the dependency and the correction route, but the symptom, causal prerequisite, waiting condition and next action are not presented as one decision path.

## 6. Evidence

EPFO FAQ 185 says Date of Exit is mandatory for online transfer; FAQs 186, 230 and 250 describe Mark Exit; FAQ 253 connects an unavailable previous Member ID to missing Date of Exit. Recent citizen reports describe the same stuck state, repeated searches, inaccessible former employers and escalation.

## 7. Severity

The citizen cannot consolidate the old PF balance until the record condition is resolved. Some cases remain self-service; employer closure, wrong dates or contribution anomalies can require escalation. Severity is high for the affected citizen, but production frequency is not established.

## 8. Scale

- **Exposed:** EPFO members who change employment and use online transfer.
- **Actually affected:** unknown; no defensible incidence figure was found.

## 9. Root cause

Process dependency hidden across service history, transfer eligibility and correction ownership—not visual styling alone.

## 10. Why existing solutions are inadequate

EPFO FAQ and search can supply instructions, but only after the citizen identifies the right prerequisite. Community advice is variable. The Member Portal owns the real record and correction; this prototype's value is the causal bridge, not a replacement portal.

## 11. Why software can improve it

A goal-specific pre-flight can test a small, published prerequisite, distinguish it from a distracting non-cause, show what not to change, and route the citizen to the correct official action before another transfer attempt.

## 12. Why this solution is materially better

The bundled journey moves from “previous account unavailable” to one evidence-linked blocker, one avoided correction, one simulated before/after, and one official handoff in two actions. It collects no identifiers and does not wait on AI.

## 13. What was rejected

- A universal cross-government identity debugger: services and rules are too heterogeneous.
- The broad Identity Rescue home with three equal scenarios: weakens the one-problem brief.
- The earlier withdrawal framing: 2026 withdrawal-rule changes make it less stable, while the transfer prerequisite is directly documented.
- A free-text rejection box: the shipped diagnosis did not use the text, so the control created false agency and sensitive-data risk.
- DigiLocker as the hero: pain is recognizable, but safe causal diagnosis would require live issuer/Aadhaar records the independent prototype cannot access.
- Handover29C as a late pivot: deeper process build, but no evidence advantage large enough to justify destroying the working, narrower EPFO slice overnight.

## 14. Confidence

| Claim | Confidence |
|---|---|
| Date of Exit is mandatory for the selected online transfer situation | HIGH |
| EPFO documents a member Mark Exit route after the waiting condition | HIGH |
| Citizens still encounter this failure | MEDIUM |
| Current UI fails to connect symptom, cause and action for most affected users | MEDIUM |
| National incidence | LOW / UNKNOWN |

## 15. Decision

**NARROW** — keep the deterministic Identity Rescue infrastructure, but make the public product one EPFO transfer-prerequisite journey. Remove the broad platform thesis and unused intake interaction from the judged path.
