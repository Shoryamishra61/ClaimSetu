## Recording controls

- Record at 1280×720 or higher with the cursor visible.
- Use one tab and rehearse once so every click lands on its assigned second.
- Do not click an outbound EPFO link during the recording.
- Keep all mock-data and no-government-connection disclosures visible when possible.
- Export at 1080p or 720p, confirm the final duration is below 2:00, then test the public link in a signed-out window.
